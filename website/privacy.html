Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 25, 2026

At CareerForge ("we", "our", or "us"), we are committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring you have a positive experience on our mobile application (the "App"). This Privacy Policy explains our practices regarding the collection, use, disclosure, and protection of your personal information when you use CareerForge.

By downloading, installing, or using the CareerForge App, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this Privacy Policy.

1. Information We Collect

We collect information you provide directly to us when you use the App:

  • Account Information: When you create an account, we collect your name, email address, and authentication credentials.
  • Profile & CV Data: We collect the information you voluntarily enter into your Master Profile or specific CVs, including your professional experience, education, skills, contact details, and any other text or images you provide for your resumes and cover letters.
  • App Usage Data: We automatically collect information about how you interact with the App, such as features used, templates selected, and app settings.
  • Device Information: We may collect device-specific information such as device type, operating system version, and unique device identifiers to improve app performance and resolve technical issues.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect for the following purposes:

  • To provide, maintain, and improve the CareerForge App and its features (e.g., CV building, ATS scoring, cover letter generation).
  • To create and manage your account.
  • To store your Master Profile and generated documents (CVs, cover letters) securely in the cloud so you can access them across your devices.
  • To communicate with you, including responding to your inquiries and sending important service updates.
  • To monitor and analyze trends, usage, and activities in connection with our App.
  • To detect, investigate, and prevent fraudulent transactions and other illegal activities and protect the rights and property of CareerForge and others.

3. Data Storage and Security

We use Supabase, a secure cloud database service, to store your account information, profile data, and generated documents. Supabase employs industry-standard security measures, including encryption at rest and in transit, to protect your data from unauthorized access, loss, or misuse.

While we strive to use commercially acceptable means to protect your personal information, remember that no method of transmission over the internet, or method of electronic storage is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee its absolute security.

4. Sharing of Information

We do not sell, trade, or rent your personal identification information to others. We may share generic aggregated demographic information not linked to any personal identification information regarding visitors and users with our business partners, trusted affiliates, and advertisers.

We may share your information with third-party service providers (like Supabase) who assist us in operating our app, conducting our business, or serving our users, so long as those parties agree to keep this information confidential.

We may also release information when its release is appropriate to comply with the law, enforce our site policies, or protect ours or others' rights, property, or safety.

5. User Rights (GDPR Compliance)

Depending on your location, you may have certain rights regarding your personal data:

  • Access: You can request a copy of the personal data we hold about you.
  • Correction: You can edit and update your personal information directly within the App.
  • Deletion: You can delete your account and all associated data from the App settings or by requesting deletion (see section below).
  • Portability: You can export your data in the form of PDF resumes and cover letters.

6. Data Retention

We will retain your personal information only for as long as is necessary for the purposes set out in this Privacy Policy. We will retain and use your information to the extent necessary to comply with our legal obligations, resolve disputes, and enforce our policies. When you delete your account, all your personal data, including CVs and profile information, is permanently deleted from our servers within 30 days.

7. Children's Privacy

CareerForge is not intended for use by children under the age of 13 (or higher age requirement as applicable in your jurisdiction). We do not knowingly collect personal identifiable information from children under 13. If a parent or guardian becomes aware that their child has provided us with personal information, they should contact us. If we become aware that we have collected personal information from a child under 13, we will take steps to remove that information from our servers.

8. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page and updating the "Last updated" date at the top. You are advised to review this Privacy Policy periodically for any changes.

9. Contact Us

If you have any questions or suggestions about our Privacy Policy, do not hesitate to contact us at: careerforge.app@gmail.com
