Account Data Deletion

At CareerForge, we respect your right to privacy and control over your personal data. This page outlines exactly how you can delete your account and what happens to your data when you do.

How to delete your account in the App

The fastest and most straightforward way to delete your account and all associated data is directly through the CareerForge mobile app:

  1. Open the CareerForge app on your mobile device.
  2. Make sure you are logged into the account you wish to delete.
  3. Navigate to the Profile tab or section.
  4. Tap on the Settings icon (usually a gear).
  5. Scroll down and tap on Delete Account.
  6. You will be prompted to confirm this action. Please read the warning carefully, as this action cannot be undone.
  7. Confirm the deletion. Your account will be immediately deactivated and queued for complete data removal.

Alternative: Request account deletion via email

If you no longer have access to the app or your device, you can request account deletion by emailing our support team:

  • Send an email to careerforge.app@gmail.com
  • Use the subject line: "Account Deletion Request"
  • Please send the email from the address associated with your CareerForge account so we can verify your identity.
  • Our team will process your request and confirm deletion within 7 business days.

What data gets deleted?

When you initiate an account deletion, we completely remove all your personal data from our active databases (hosted securely on Supabase). This includes:

  • Your account credentials (email, name).
  • Your Master Profile data (experience, education, skills, contact info).
  • All saved resumes/CVs and their specific configurations.
  • All generated cover letters.
  • Any uploaded profile images or assets.

Data Deletion Timeline

Upon confirming account deletion, your profile becomes immediately inaccessible. All personal data listed above is permanently and irrevocably deleted from our production servers and backups within 30 days of the request. After this period, no trace of your personal information will remain in our systems.

Please Note: Before deleting your account, we highly recommend exporting your resumes and cover letters as PDFs to your device, as they cannot be recovered once your account is deleted.
